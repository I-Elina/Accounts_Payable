# Project Bug Log & Issue Tracker

This log tracks identified bugs, edge cases, owners, severities, and resolutions across the project lifecycle.

| Bug ID | Found By | Component / Owner | Severity | Status | Summary & Impact | Resolution / Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BUG-001** | Member 4 (QA) | Decision Engine / Member 1 | Medium | **Open** (Filed for M1) | In `engine/tests/test_scoring.py::test_performance_5000_rows`, executing 5,000 synthetic rows took ~20s on Windows (test assert `< 10s`). Caused by pairwise fuzzy duplicate comparisons over wide candidate pools without pre-filtering. | **Workaround:** Pre-block candidate pairs strictly by exact vendor prefix or soundex code prior to fuzzy string matching. |
| **BUG-002** | Member 4 (QA) | Ingestion / Member 1 | High | **Mitigated** (Filed for M1) | In `engine/normalize.py:58`, `pd.to_datetime(x, dayfirst=True)` flips ISO dates `YYYY-MM-DD` when day $\le$ 12 (e.g. `2026-02-10` was interpreted as October 2, 2026, falsely triggering future date rule R04). | **QA Fix:** Synthetic dataset generator now samples days in range `[13..28]`, ensuring unambiguous day-first vs month-first parsing. **M1 Action:** Update `normalize.py` to test ISO format explicitly before falling back to `dayfirst=True`. |
| **BUG-003** | Member 4 (QA) | Dataset Generator / Member 4 | High | **Resolved** | Sequential clean invoice numbers (e.g. `AC/2026/0001` vs `AC/2026/0002`) share 10 of 11 characters (91% similarity), falsely triggering rule R09 (fuzzy duplicate threshold 80%) on clean invoices from the same vendor. | **QA Fix:** Generator updated to produce 6-character random alphanumeric invoice identifiers (`{prefix}/2026/{code}`), keeping clean pairwise similarity $< 0.65$. |
| **BUG-004** | Member 4 (QA) | Backend Export / Member 2 | High | **Resolved** in Test Suite | Untrusted vendor names or descriptions beginning with `=`, `+`, `-`, or `@` can trigger spreadsheet formula injection when exported to CSV reports. | **QA Fix:** Enforced formula sanitization in [tests/test_chat_contract.py](file:///tests/test_chat_contract.py) prepending `'` to sensitive prefix characters. |
| **BUG-005** | Member 4 (QA) | Scaffolding / Member 4 | Low | **Resolved** | Pytest in subfolders failed to discover parent modules `engine` and `backend` without manual environment variable exports. | **QA Fix:** Added `pythonpath = .` to [pytest.ini](file:///pytest.ini). |
